# 🟢 equipdash-equipdash

> EquipDash is a rental, booking, and experience management platform for equipment rental businesses. The MCP server provides 123 tools organized across 24 domains:

- Bookings: Create, list, update, cancel bookings; manage notes and tags; track late pickups/returns
- Products: Manage rental products, check availability, calculate pricing, track inventory
- Experiences: Manage bookable experiences, check availability, view schedules
- Customers: Full customer management with search, notes, and custom emails
- Payments: Record payments, process Stripe refunds, apply promo codes/vouchers, send payment links
- Staff: Manage staff members, create/edit shifts, view schedules
- Store: Manage retail/store products, adjust inventory, track sales
- Reports: Revenue summaries, booking stats/trends, inventory reports, top customers/products
- Promotions: Create and validate promo codes and vouchers
- Forms & Surveys: Manage waivers/forms and survey analytics
- Categories & Tags: Organize products, experiences, and store items
- Bundles: Check bundle availability
- Campaigns: View campaign stats
- Waitlist: Manage waitlist entries
- Workshop: Track items in workshop/repair
- Seasons: Manage seasonal pricing
- Settings: View business hours and locations
- Support: Create and manage support tickets
- AI Agents: Run and manage automated agents
