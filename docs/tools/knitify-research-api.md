# 🟡 knitify-research-api

> PubMed research tools for AI agents — 3 tools, structured JSON output, verified citations.                                                                                                                         
1. research — Ask any health or science question and get a fully cited research article backed by real PubMed papers with PMIDs.                      
2. analyze_product — Point to any health product URL and get a PubMed-backed evidence analysis of its ingredients and claims. Perfect for science-backed product research, marketing, and commercialization.
3. find_clinical_evidence — For any health research question, get a research report backed by PubMed literature and recruiting clinical trials from ClinicalTrials.gov relevant to your target conditions or interventions.

All tools return typed JSON via outputSchema — not raw abstracts. 7 output languages. Credit-based pricing.
