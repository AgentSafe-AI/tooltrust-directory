# 🟢 aisle-wedding

> Search destination wedding venues and vendors worldwide with Aisle. 10 tools for AI-assisted wedding planning:
  - Venue Search: Find wedding venues by country, type (villa, beach, castle, resort), capacity, and budget. Covers 20+ countries.
  - Vendor Search: Browse wedding photographers, florists, planners, DJs, caterers, and 10 more categories by location and price range.
  - Budget Estimator: Get a detailed wedding cost breakdown by guest count, destination, and style tier (budget to luxury). Covers 23 countries.
  - Planning Timeline: Month-by-month destination wedding checklist from 18 months out to wedding day.
  - Packing Lists: Climate and role-specific packing lists for brides, grooms, and guests.
  - Planning Guides: Search 11 expert guides on budgets, legal requirements, etiquette, RSVPs, and more.
