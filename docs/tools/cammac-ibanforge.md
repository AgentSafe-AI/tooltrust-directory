# 🟢 cammac-ibanforge

> IBAN validation and bank-data tools for AI agents, ERP, invoicing and payroll workflows. IBANforge covers 89 countries and provides BIC/SWIFT lookup, Swiss clearing lookup, SEPA and country-risk indicators, payment-reference validation, postal-address checks and Swiss QR-bill checks. The current MCP service offers 11 tools, including batch validation, feedback and API-key requests. Connect to https://api.ibanforge.com/mcp or use the ibanforge-mcp npm package. Free access: 25 calls per day without a key, 25 per month with an anonymous key, or 200 per month after claiming a key by email. Paid credit packs, a Pro subscription and x402 payments are available. Batch validation consumes one unit per IBAN. Validation checks format and checksum; it does not confirm that an account exists or belongs to a beneficiary, and it does not perform beneficiary AML/KYC screening. Documentation: https://ibanforge.com/docs
