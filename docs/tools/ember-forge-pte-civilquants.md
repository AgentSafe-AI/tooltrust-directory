# 🟢 ember-forge-pte-civilquants

> CivilQuants computes audit-traceable Bills of Quantities for civil-engineering assemblies — culverts, manholes, retaining walls, pavements, drainage, earthworks — across four UK measurement standards (CESMM4, NRM2, SMM7, MMHW). Parametric inputs in; a methodology-structured BoQ with every quantity traceable to its formula out, exportable to Excel, DXF and PDF. Paid subscribers also get 10 senior-QS methodology prompts (tender review, risk assessment, measurement and contract advice, geotechnical interpretation and more). Six assemblies are free and anonymous.
