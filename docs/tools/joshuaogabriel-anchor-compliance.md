# 🟢 joshuaogabriel-anchor-compliance

> Australian advertising & marketing compliance for AI assistants. Scan websites, check ad/social/email copy, and look up regulator guidelines (ACCC, TGA, ASIC, AHPRA, ACMA) directly from Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor.
