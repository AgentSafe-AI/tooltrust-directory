# 🟠 narrateai-video

> Stop recording your voice. NarrateAI adds professional voiceover to silent screen recordings — automatically. Just drop a video URL and get a narrated demo, dubbed video, or polished document back. Works from Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, or any MCP client.

**What it does:**
- Narrates silent videos with AI voiceover — no mic, no script writing, no editing
- 7 AI voices + voice cloning from a 15-second audio sample
- Transcribes speech-to-text from any video (meetings, podcasts, tutorials)
- Translates and dubs videos into any language with cloned speaker voice
- Converts screen recordings into professional docs (5 templates: onboarding guides, tutorials, feature showcases, product docs, business overviews)
- Text-to-speech for any text

**18 MCP tools** — single videos, batch processing (up to 5), transcript editing, voice cloning, TTS, and more.

Free tier: 5 minutes of video processing. Upgrade anytime at [narrateai.app](https://narrateai.app).
