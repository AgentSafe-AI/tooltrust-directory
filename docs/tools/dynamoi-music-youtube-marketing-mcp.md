# 🟡 dynamoi-music-youtube-marketing-mcp

> Music marketing for AI agents. Connect ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, and other AI agents to Dynamoi for free Spotify Smart Links, music promotion campaigns, analytics, and distribution. Dynamoi is a music marketing platform for artists, labels, and managers.

- **Smart Links:** create free Spotify Smart Links and artist hubs, preview the four page themes, and read link analytics.
- **Promotion campaigns:** check the status and performance of managed Meta and YouTube campaigns, and launch, adjust, pause, or resume them after you confirm.
- **Platform setup:** check Spotify, Meta, and YouTube connection health and what is left to set up.
- **Music distribution:** check the five published requirements and submit an application for manual review.

Sign in with your Dynamoi account over OAuth; every result respects your roles and artist access. Agents can learn what Dynamoi is before signing in with the `dynamoi_about` tool.

Docs: https://dynamoi.com/docs/mcp-server
