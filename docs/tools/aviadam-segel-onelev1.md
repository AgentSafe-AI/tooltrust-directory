> # 1lev1 (1💗1)

Welcome to **1lev1**, a collaborative partnership platform designed to foster deep alignment, non-violence, and shared success. 

At its core, 1lev1 moves beyond traditional governance models. Instead of relying on a democratic majority where some voices are inevitably overridden, the platform is built entirely around the principle of **unanimous agreement**. Every decision, contract, and consensus is reached through active negotiation until all parties are fully aligned.

## 🌟 Core Features

* **Weaves (רקמות):** A dynamic approach to task management and collaboration. Weaves allow users to seamlessly interconnect ideas, responsibilities, and revenue-sharing in a natural, organic workflow.
* **Magic Meetings:** A frictionless scheduling tool designed to bring people together effortlessly, eliminating the usual back-and-forth of coordinating times.
* **Global Agreements:** Serving as the engine behind initiatives like *agreement.1lev1.com*, the platform acts as a hub for establishing trust and affirming core human values on a global scale.

## 🎨 Our Visual Identity
The philosophy of the platform is directly reflected in its design. In the **1💗1** logo, the "1" on the left is intentionally mirrored so that its tip points inward toward the center heart. This symbolizes two distinct, equal sides coming together to form a unified, harmonious whole.

## 🤖 AI-Powered Collaboration (MCP)
1lev1 is ready for the future of agentic workflows. Through the official [`1lev1-mcp`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/1lev1-mcp) package, developers can connect their favorite AI assistants directly to the platform. 

Give your AI agent the ability to:
* Read and manage your Weaves.
* Schedule and organize Magic Meetings.
* Assist in facilitating negotiations to help teams reach unanimous consensus.
