# 🟡 frazierlee-onesocial

> OneSocial — The Professional Operating System™

Find vetted freelancers, creatives, and service professionals with verified credibility scores. OneSocial answers the question every client asks: "If I hire you, will you actually show up and do a great job?"

What you can do:
🔍 Search professionals by category, location, and minimum credibility score (OneScore™)
⭐ Check reviews from verified, completed jobs (1–7 star scale)
📊 Get credibility breakdowns across 7 dimensions: Profile, Social, Media, Jobs, Reviews, Endorsements & VEA
📅 Browse events and check professional availability
📝 Digital contracts — send and manage agreements without DocuSign
🎯 AI lead generation — find ideal clients using smart targeting
🏟️ The Arena — watch professionals compete head-to-head for credibility
🤖 VAIA — AI career assistant for profile optimization and growth
Replaces:
Fiverr • Upwork • LinkedIn search • DocuSign • Eventbrite • Google Calendar for freelancers
